# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We want Modeldiffr to be a welcoming project for everyone, regardless of background, experience level, identity, or location. We commit to making participation a harassment free experience.

## Expected behavior

* Be respectful and patient, especially with newcomers.
* Give and accept constructive feedback about work, not people.
* Assume good intent, and ask before assuming.
* Credit the work and ideas of others.
* Keep discussions focused on what is best for the project and its users.

## Unacceptable behavior

* Harassment, insults, or personal attacks of any kind.
* Discriminatory language or imagery.
* Publishing others' private information without permission.
* Sustained disruption of discussions.
* Any conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Scope

This code applies in all project spaces, including issues, pull requests, discussions, and any event or channel where someone represents the project.

## Enforcement

Report concerns to hebbar.gvamaresh@gmail.com. All reports are reviewed promptly and handled confidentially. Maintainers may remove, edit, or reject contributions, and may temporarily or permanently ban participants whose behavior violates this code.
